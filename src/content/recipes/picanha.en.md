Refrigerate until needed.", "Prepare a grill with a hot direct zone and a cooler indirect zone.", "Slice the picanha into 3 cm steaks, keeping the fat cap. Pat dry and season with salt.", "Grill over direct heat for 3–4 minutes per side. Finish over indirect heat until the center reaches 63°C.", "Rest for at least 3 minutes and serve with herb butter."]
